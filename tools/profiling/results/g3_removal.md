## Method

- Arms: `O_norc` (W1 with the `O_norc` output set, baseline), `nrr` (`O_norc` with `--no-enable-rerouting`), `nss` and `nst` (`O_norc` with the in-process stubs below). One interleaved `abba` block `G3_rm_W1`: warm-up 1 and 5 reps per arm; counters block `G3_ctr_W1`: 2 reps per arm, no warm-up, for agent-steps. The `nss` row comes from the rerun blocks `G3b_rm_W1` and `G3b_ctr_W1` (`O_norc` and `nss` only, same settings, `G3_PASS=G3b G3_ARMS=O_norc,nss`), compared with the `O_norc` of `G3b_rm_W1`. All arms enter through `instrument/perf_run.py` (`--via-wrapper`); the stubs live in the harness (D36) and the frozen pyFDS-Evac worktree is unchanged.
- `nss` (#242): `SliceFieldSampler.sample` calls `self._time_index(float(time_s))` and returns a neutral value of its quantity: 0.209 for the oxygen volume fraction, 0.0 for every other quantity (D36). The first arm returned 0.0 for every quantity and is invalid (see #242 above); the rerun uses the corrected stub. The horizon rule and its `FdsHorizonError` are kept, and the data read, `_find_subslice` and `_nearest_index` are skipped; the `ValueError` that the real sampler raises for a point outside the slice is not raised either, which no W1 run triggered. Patched on the class, so the smoke-speed, FED and heat samplers all use it. The route line-of-sight reads the slice as before.
- `nst` (#429): `reached_stage` is `False` for no target, else `hypot(x − target[0], y − target[1]) ≤ float(agent_radius) + TARGET_REACH_MARGIN_M`. Patched on `pyfds_evac.core.scenario` and `pyfds_evac.core.direct_steering_runtime`, because `scenario` binds the name at import.
- Verdict rule (D37, design decision 6): `diff_s = median(O_norc) − median(arm)` over usable reps (termination `completed`, `flows_closed` or `incomplete_time_limit`, `claude` offenders excluded); `tie` when `|diff_s|` is at most the larger IQR of the two arms; otherwise `fix_first` when `diff_s × 10` exceeds 23 s, else `below_bar`. The code applies the rule; this report copies the `verdict` column and never overrides it. No arm is `tie` or `below_bar`. The interquartile range is the exclusive-method `statistics.quantiles` (n=4), which on five reps is wider than the inclusive method (`O_norc` 1.555 s against 0.823 s per run); this is conservative for the tie rule and changes no verdict.
- Flagged reps (D34): kept in the medians and counted. No rep is excluded.
- What the numbers are not: per-subsystem costs (arms are never subtracted from each other, and each arm changes trajectories), realistic savings from a code fix, or anything about W3, W1r or W4flow.

## What this report does not say

- It does not say what a fix saves. A `fix_first` verdict says that removing the mechanism, with the side effects of the stub, saves more than 23 s per ten-run study on W1.
- It does not rank the three measured arms as additive costs; the sum of the three differences is not a total, because the arms were never run together and the `nss` arm ends the simulation earlier.
- It does not cover W3, W1r, W4flow or any workload other than W1, and it does not cover the route-cost history output (`O_study` against `O_norc`), which is context from G1b.
- It does not isolate the cost of slice reading inside `nss` from the earlier end of the run (31,353 of 40,000 iterations); the per-agent-step column reduces but does not remove that effect. The G2 counters scaling (`G2_ctr_scale`, loop time against agent-steps at N = 100, 200, 400 and 40,000 iterations) gives a per-iteration fixed cost near zero, so the 8,647 extra `O_norc` tail iterations with 1 to 4 agents cost well under 1 s and the per-agent-step correction is fair.
- Eleven of the 32 `G3` runs and eleven of the 16 `G3b` runs are flagged by macOS daemons and applications. The table keeps them (D34); four of the five `nst` reps and all five reps of each `G3b` arm are flagged, and the table was not rerun without the flagged reps. In `G3b` the clean warm-ups give the same sign and size (`O_norc` 35.00 s, `nss` 29.56 s, 5.44 s), but warm-ups are not reps and enter no median.
