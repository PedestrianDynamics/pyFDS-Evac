## Method

- Profiled share to plain cost: `cost_plain_s = cum_s × plain_wall_s / profiled_wall_s`, with the medians of the same block (W1: plain 46.94 s, profiled 105.99 s). This assumes the profiler slows every frame by the same factor. pyinstrument adds time per Python call and the route evaluation is call-dense, so the assumption most likely over-charges the call-dense frames (#243, #242) and their `cost_plain_s` leans towards an over-estimate; the plain reps bound the total distortion, not its distribution.
- Overhead bound = `profiled_wall_s − plain_wall_s` of the block: W1 59.05 s, W3 10.67 s, W1r 16.53 s, W4flow 34.13 s. The profiled reps run in the profiler venv and the plain reps in the baseline venv; that difference is inside the bound. The overhead is far above the 10–40 % the harness notes expected (+126 % on W1).
- Per study = ×10: ten seeds of W1 are ten fresh processes and W1 has no cross-process cache. The bar is 5 % of the study wall, 23 s. A verdict applies it to the plain-wall difference of a removal experiment × 10, never to a profiled share; `(cost_plain_s − overhead_bound_s) × 10` is listed for completeness and is not a verdict.
- Tight vs loose: tight means a removal experiment measured the saving in plain walls (the route-cost history, `O_norc`); loose means a profiled share only. A loose share is attribution evidence and never a verdict (D32, amended); a tie inside the 3–4 % session shift and the within-block IQR (0.1–0.5 s on W1) is reported as a tie.
- pyinstrument sees Python frames only. Native time lands on the calling Python frame: NumPy under `fds_sampling.sample` (self time 2.42 s of 14.22 s cum on W1), `_nearest_index` (self 2.97 s of 7.38 s), and shapely under `distance_to_polygon` (the `shapely/` rows of `steering_exit_test`). Those frames are charged to #242 and #429 as a whole; the report cannot separate NumPy or shapely time from the calling function's own bytecode.
- cProfile inflates small-function costs and is used here for location only (`rc_diff_G2_rc_cprof.csv`); the magnitude of the route-cost history is the G1 removal experiment.
- Agreement rule: `agreement_pts` is the spread (max − min) over the profiled reps of `cum_s / wall_s` in points; a block with more than 2 points on any of the ten largest subsystem-mapped function rows of the CSV (by `cum_s`; 21 such rows on W1, not the ten largest frames of the whole pyinstrument tree) is inconclusive. A blank `agreement_pts` (one profiled rep) is not assessable and is never read as agreement. W4flow has one profiled rep, so its agreement is not assessable and its column is blank.
- A subsystem with no frame on a workload is `not_observed` with zero seconds; no estimate is substituted.

## What this report does not say

- Realistic savings. They need a fix design; every number above is an upper bound or a measured cost.
- Subsystem costs below the sampling resolution (1 ms) or below the within-block spread.
- Kernel and thermal effects (D10): no sudo, `kernel_task` and throttling are not observed.
- Anything about the issues' original figures (D23).
- A lower bound on any single issue: the conservative saving is negative everywhere because the profiler overhead exceeds each cost; a profiler-free removal experiment is the only way to get one.
