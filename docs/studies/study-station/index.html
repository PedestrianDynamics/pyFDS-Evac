      Station nightclub fire (exit use, a failed test)

      Station nightclub fire: coupled exit choice against survivor exit use

      We ran coupled pyFDS-Evac simulations of the 2003 Station nightclub fire and compared the exit use of the simulated survivors with survivor exit-use data (Fahy, Proulx & Flynn 2011, Table 2). The comparison was pre-registered: the criteria were frozen before the first FDS fire run.

      Every scored gate fails. V1 fails, V2 fails and V3, the primary claim, fails. V4 to V6 were not scored. The registered wording for the primary claim applies: “coupling adds no measurable skill to exit choice here”.

      This page publishes the failure under the pre-registration’s failure policy (§8): “A failed criterion is published as a disclosed failure”. Nothing was tuned afterwards.

      ℹ️
      The results come from the tag station-prereg-v1, commit aaeda1f3. The page reports one scenario run with research software, compared with survivor exit-use data. It gives no design or safety verdict. It does not validate the model, reconstruct the event or count deaths; see What this page does not claim.

      Spreads are min / median / max over the 20 scored seeds, as validate.py reports them. Each row covers 6660 placed survivor-agents (333 × 20).

      • The front-door closure moves T1 most. Arm C gives 58.8 %; closing the front door at 86 s (C+jam) gives 40.4 %.
      • W is higher with coupling on the same agents. R is worse than C+jam in every seed of the primary row.
      • The baseline already misses W. C and C+jam have W ≥ 0.30, against a band of 0.15 and a noise floor of 0.08 to 0.11. This gap exists before any fire enters, and its cause is not studied here.
      • No variant or sensitivity row passes V3. The closest is the −15 s fire: R lower in 6 of 20 seeds, mean +0.004.

      What was registered

      The freeze is the tag station-prereg-v1, archived on Zenodo as 10.5281/zenodo.24190605. The frozen files are in assets/station_prereg/: preregistration.md, freeze_record.md and SHA256SUMS. All 20 checksums were OK before scoring (shasum -a 256 -c assets/station_prereg/SHA256SUMS). The reproduction commands (build, run, scoring) are in freeze_record.md. Versions: JuPedSim 1.4.2, fdsvismap 0.3.3, fdsreader 1.14.0, Python 3.12.13, FDS 6.10.1 (FDS-6.10.1-0-g12efa16-release).

      The run records (635 runs), the 34 scorer outputs, the score summary and the look log are archived at doi:10.5281/zenodo.24226552. The trajectory files are not included.

      Targets.

      • T1: the front-door share among door users, placed rows 117 of 229 = 51.1 % (Fahy, Proulx & Flynn 2011, Table 2).
      • W: the door-user-weighted total variation distance of the exit matrix by starting area. Per row it is ½ Σ |model − Fahy| over the doors, weighted by Fahy’s door users. Fahy resampled against itself gives a noise floor with median 0.079 and 95th percentile 0.109.

      Arms.

      ArmConditions
      CClear air, no closure
      C+jamClear air, front door closes at 86 s
      UFire, smoke-blind, jam
      RCoupled, jam

      The coupling is described in routing (w_queue) and the smoke-speed model.

      Run design. N = 455 agents per run: 333 placed survivors, 22 unplaceable survivors and 100 victims. Victims and unplaceable agents are excluded from T1 and W. Seeds 4 to 23 are scored (20 seeds); pilots 1 to 3 are not. The horizon is 250 s (234 s for the −15 s variant). t = 0 is first flame. Pre-movement times are uniform bands after Galea et al. (2008, p. 473). w_queue = 0.

      Criteria.

      #Registered criterionRole
      V1Arm C: T1 in 44.5 to 57.7 %, W ≤ 0.15Baseline; not blind
      V2Arm R, central fire: same bandsPrimary
      V3W(R) − W(C+jam) per seed, same agents: R lower in at least 15 of 20 seeds and mean difference at least 0.03, central fire onlyPrimary claim
      V3mW(R) − W(U) per seed, same agents: sign count, no bandMechanism test

      Same agents means the agents that reached a door in both arms at that seed (validate.py --against).

      What had been seen before scoring (pre-registration §6). The registration is not blind throughout, and the page says where.

      • V1 was not blind. Stage 1 front-door shares of 31 to 34 %, a w_queue sweep and the V0 end times had been seen.
      • V2 stayed marked blind, but the end line of the unscored pilot seed 1 (331 of 455 evacuated) had been seen.
      • w_queue = 0 was chosen after the V0 looks.
      • The 0.024 row is a fit to Fahy (53.0 % against Fahy’s 52.9 %, the T1′ share that includes the unplaceable rows) and is labelled so.

This page reports that definition and does not change it. “Never reached a door” (1531) differs from FED ≥ 1 (1885): 354 agents have FED ≥ 1 and still count as having reached a door. The reason is not established.

      How to read them

      • T1 shares cover different agent sets per arm. The low T1 of V2 alone is not evidence about coupling.
      • W is higher with coupling on the same agents. V3 compares R and C+jam on the agents that reached a door in both runs of a seed. R is worse in every seed of the primary row and in at least 14 of 20 seeds of every other row (the fewest is the −15 s row, 14 of 20).
      • Switching off one part leaves the failure. With anticipation off (R-na, +0.075) or the incapacitation stop off (dose only, +0.072), V3 still fails. This shows that disabling either one alone does not remove the failure. It does not show that neither contributes. Dose only also censors 15.1 %.
      • The finding is no measurable skill gain. The page does not claim a mechanism by which coupling lowers the match.

      Agents left inside at the end of R runs

      ⚠️
      This whole section is a post-score look, outside the pre-registration and unscored. The run-end counts cover all R runs; the counterfactual table is one seed (primary central fire, seed 04). It is recorded in the look log and changes no registered number.

      How R runs end. Every R run exits with code 2 (the run ended with agents still inside). Apart from the 20 dose-only runs, which reach the time limit, R runs end with all_mobile_left. In all R runs together, 27,193 of the 27,193 agents still inside at the end have FED ≥ 1. Of these, 15,160 moved less than 0.5 m in the 10 s before FED reached 1. The deadlock detector fired in four C+jam runs only, with 2 agents each.

      What changes the count (seed 04).

      RunWhat differs from RFED ≥ 1End
      R (frozen)none125all_mobile_left, 103 s
      R, bodies removed (diagnostic)incapacitated agents removed instead of held in place125all agents gone
      X1 (exploratory)smoke speed factor fixed at 189all_mobile_left, 97 s
      U (frozen)smoke-blind: no slowdown, no rerouting, no incapacitation106, crossing between 72 and 91 sall exit

      The smoke speed law is Lund with floor 0.1 (smoke-speed model). Fixing the factor at 1 lowers the count from 125 to 89 in this seed. The mechanism, a longer stay in the dose, is a reading, not tested.

      What the evidence supports: the stops are dominated by dose in the queue region, present in every fire arm, with a smaller share from the smoke speed law. It does not exclude a contribution of the movement model (queue speed under WarpDriver) to the time spent in the dose. These are single-seed counterfactuals.

      What this page does not claim

      • That the model is validated. The page compares the model with survivor exit-use data.
      • A forensic reconstruction of the event.
      • Death counts or survival.
      • FED or visibility as validated, or FED compared with deaths by area (a pre-freeze decision).
      • Any fire-time shift as a match. The ±15 s rows are an envelope.
      • Novelty beyond smoke-dependent exit choice.

      Known bias. CO and HCN yields as published leave a 34 % per-MJ shortfall against Purser’s 14.9 MJ/kg, so FED is biased low.

      Inputs. The geometry station_nist is “provenance unverified”: the NCSTAR 2 App. L.2.2 deck, transcribed. Victim placement is an assumption; Spearpoint (2012) Table 1 gives no recovery sites. The fire field was not checked against NIST: the fire is model against model at best, because V6 was not scored.

      Owed and not yet produced

      These items are required by the pre-registration and tracked in #950.

      • V4: first-target front-door share.
      • V5: 0.9 to 1.5 person/s through the 914 mm door.
      • V6: fire-side check, T5 in 65 to 100 s and T6 in 65 to 115 s. It needs fire slices that nobody has opened.
      • The §2 survival rates under Fahy resampling.
      • The §3 heat-only incapacitation, raised-floor FED and K (#365 is open), and the vestibule count at 86 s.
      • Censored shares and t_jam splits beside every sensitivity row. They are in score files 08 to 34 and not tabulated here.

      The frozen tree has no scorer for these. The pre-registration forbids tuning, not implementing frozen analyses. None of them is a result of this page.

      Deviations and history

      • Pre-movement, w_queue and t_jam rows have no arm-C build. They were scored for C+jam, U and R only.
      • R-na and dose-only were paired with the primary C+jam and U runs (the analyst’s choice). The same-agent check passed.
      • Arm C was scored without the t_jam split.
      • The look order was V1, V2, V3, V3m, context arms, fire variants, sensitivities. The analyst chose it; the pre-registration sets none.
      • The arm C-NIST row and the N = 440 and N = 462 rows were dropped before the freeze.
      • The look log is public in the data record (doi:10.5281/zenodo.24226552).

      Limits

      • One scenario, 20 seeds, a 0.2 m FDS grid and one realisation per fire. The page draws no general conclusion from it.
      • WarpDriver at the 0.52 to 0.77 m openings: jupedsim#1720 and jupedsim#1719.
      • Heat never reaches route choice: rank_routes takes the gas dose only.
      • Raised floors are open (#365).
